Drop rules

  • Every drop row gives exactly 1 piece; rows roll independently, so one kill can give several items.
  • Monsters drop no coins. Coins come from selling loot, quests and the like.
  • Cards drop at 0.05%. A boss's own card, and the card of each minion it calls (in the boss's table), drop at 0.5%.
  • An Elite kill rolls every card row at ×3 (0.05% → 0.15%) and every equipment row at ×2.5, plus the Elite bonus table.
  • The world's server rates multiply chances only (Drop for items, Card for cards), up to 100%, never the number of pieces.
  • Rarity: monster-dropped equipment rolls its rarity and options, up to Epic from normal monsters and Elites, Legendary only from the designated MVPs: Dewroot Regent. Shop items never roll.
  • Only what the live game can reach is listed: maps no portal leads to, their monsters, and items nothing gives yet stay out until they join the game.
